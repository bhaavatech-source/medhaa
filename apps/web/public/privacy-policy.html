Privacy Policy for Individual Games

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy is for standalone Android games published by Bhāva Tech. It is intended to be the privacy-policy URL used by those games in their Google Play listings. It does not cover the Medhā website, account-based services, or other Bhāva Tech products; those services have separate notices, including the Medhā Usage & Data Policy.

Each game may use this policy only when its actual data practices match the disclosures below. We will add a game-specific disclosure here before releasing a standalone game that handles data differently.

Games covered and their data practices

GameData handled by the game
SmṛtiThe current standalone version does not collect, store, or transmit personal information or gameplay data. Gameplay runs on the device. It has no account, advertising, or analytics feature.

Other individual game apps are covered only after they are listed here with accurate, game-specific disclosures. If a game adds sign-in, cloud saves, score sharing, analytics, advertising, or another connected feature, this policy and that game's Play Store disclosures must be updated before release.

Information, storage, and sharing

For Smṛti, Bhāva Tech does not receive gameplay, scores, device identifiers, contact details, or other personal information from the app. The game does not save a player profile or send game activity to our servers. We do not sell or share information collected by Smṛti because it does not collect such information.

Google Play and Android may independently process information related to app distribution, device operation, or diagnostics according to their own settings and privacy policies. That processing is not performed by Smṛti.

Children and families

Smṛti is a family-friendly memory game. It does not ask players to create an account or provide personal information, and it does not include third-party ads or analytics in its current standalone version. Parents or guardians can contact us with questions about a game or this policy.

Retention, deletion, and security

Because Smṛti does not transmit or retain personal or gameplay data, Bhāva Tech has no Smṛti player data to retrieve or delete. We use reasonable care to keep the app and its distribution materials secure. Any future game feature that changes these practices will be disclosed before that version is released.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when a game changes or our practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated. The policy linked from a game's Google Play listing should describe the version currently available to players.
