Privacy Policy & Terms of Use — Medhā (Bhāva Tech)

What we collect: participant name, age, optional email/school, and (for minors) parent/guardian name and relationship, entered directly into this form. We also record in-session responses, timings, and page-visibility (integrity) events for scoring purposes only.

Where your data goes: this assessment runs entirely in your browser. Your responses are not sent to Bhāva Tech's servers unless you explicitly click "Download Data" and share that file with us yourself, or unless this deployment has been configured with an active submission endpoint (if so, it is disclosed to you before you begin).

Children's data: for participants aged 5–17, we require a parent/legal guardian to provide their name as an e-signature and to confirm their relationship to the child before any activity begins, in line with Section 9 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not use children's data for behavioural tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising.

Retention & deletion: if no submission endpoint is configured, no copy of your data is retained by Bhāva Tech at all — it exists only on your device. If you have shared a downloaded report or JSON file with us directly, you may request its deletion at any time by emailing contact@bhavatech.in.

Not a clinical tool: Medhā is an educational prototype. Scores reflect relative in-session performance only and are not a validated IQ test, clinical diagnosis, or educational placement decision.
