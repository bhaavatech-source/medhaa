Privacy Policy & Terms of Use — Medhā (Bhāva Tech)

What we collect: participant name, age, optional email/school, and (for minors) parent/guardian name and relationship, entered directly into this form. The assessment also stores a random browser-level assessment key and anonymous item-variant history locally to reduce repeated assessment items. We also record in-session responses, timings, and page-visibility (integrity) events for scoring purposes only.

Where your data goes: this assessment runs in your browser and, when online, submits the assessment record to Bhāva Tech's Medhā API for result records and payment-reference processing. The assessment uses the participant details entered on the page, responses, timings and integrity events for this purpose.

Children's data: for participants aged 5–17, we require a parent/legal guardian to provide their name as an e-signature and to confirm their relationship to the child before any activity begins, in line with Section 9 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We do not use children's data for behavioural tracking, profiling, or targeted advertising.

Retention & deletion: assessment records submitted to Bhāva Tech are stored in the Medhā assessment system. You may request deletion of your assessment record or any copy you shared with us by emailing contact@bhavatech.in.

Not a clinical tool: Medhā is an educational prototype. Scores reflect relative in-session performance only and are not a validated IQ test, clinical diagnosis, or educational placement decision.
