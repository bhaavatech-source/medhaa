👋 Welcome to Dual Phone Anatomy!

We've split the phone in half! On the left is the front glass and screen you touch every day. On the right is the intense, high-tech engine room hiding underneath. Click around!

⚡ OVERCLOCK MODE ACTIVATED!

Maximum processing power engaged! The phone is working at lightning speed!

📺 OLED Display

Millions of microscopic LEDs that light up to show your games and videos. The touch-sensitive layer (digitizer) is glued right to it!

🛡️ Gorilla Glass

Chemically hardened glass designed to survive drops and prevent scratches from the keys in your pocket.

👆 Under-Display Fingerprint

A hidden camera or ultrasonic sensor looking right through the screen to map the microscopic ridges of your thumb!

👁️ Sensor Notch

The command center for the front! It hides the selfie camera, the earpiece speaker, and the infrared dot-projector used to unlock the phone with your face.

📱 Chassis & Frame

The tough metal skeleton. Notice the tiny gold screws that hold the motherboard down tightly.

🧩 Mainboard

The electronic puzzle board. It is densely packed with tiny silver capacitors and logic gates to control the electricity.

🧠 CPU (The Brain)

The super-smart brain of the phone. It solves billions of math problems every second to run your intense 3D apps.

🚀 RAM Memory

The phone's short-term memory! It holds exactly what you're doing right now so apps open instantly without waiting.

💾 ROM Storage

The giant digital backpack. This is where all your apps, operating systems, and permanent files live.

💳 SIM Card

The tiny gold chip that holds your secret phone number and securely connects you to the cell towers for calls and 5G internet!

💽 MicroSD Card

An extra tiny, removable memory card you can slide in to store thousands of more photos, videos, and games.

🔋 Li-Ion Battery

The giant power plant taking up most of the space inside! It feeds raw electricity to every single part.

📸 Rear Cameras

Multiple robot eyes! Some see super wide, some zoom in really far, and all work together to take perfect pictures.

🔊 Loudspeaker

A tiny box pushing air super fast to create loud music, funny video sounds, and alarm bells.

🔌 USB-C Port

The main gate! Used to blast the battery full of electricity or connect the phone to other computers.

🎧 Headphone Jack (3.5mm)

The classic analog audio port! It lets you plug in wired headphones to listen to music with zero lag and without needing batteries.

📳 Taptic Engine

A little motor with a heavy weight that shakes back and forth really fast to create vibrations and fake button clicks!

🎗️ Flex Cables & Copper

Gold ribbons transmit data, while the orange copper heat pipe moves heat away from the CPU so it doesn't melt!
