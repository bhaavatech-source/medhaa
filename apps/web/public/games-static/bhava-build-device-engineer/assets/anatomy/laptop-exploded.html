⚡ OVERCLOCK MODE ACTIVATED!

Maximum processing power engaged! The fans are spinning up and the laptop is working at lightning speed!

📺 Display Panel

A beautiful high-resolution screen (like IPS or OLED) made of millions of tiny lights to show your games, code, and movies perfectly.

📷 HD Webcam

A tiny high-definition camera built right into the top bezel so you can join video calls or stream your games.

🎙️ Array Microphones

Multiple tiny microphones spaced apart. They work together to hear your voice clearly while ignoring background noise like clicking keyboards!

🛡️ Lid Bezel & Chassis

The tough outer frame (usually aluminum or plastic) that protects the fragile glass screen from bending and snapping in your backpack.

💻 Aluminum Base

The heavy bottom case of the laptop. It acts as armor, keeping all the expensive chips safe while also helping absorb heat.

🧩 Motherboard

The giant electronic city grid! Every single component plugs into this board so they can send electricity and data to each other.

🧠 CPU (Processor)

The main brain of the computer. It handles all the logic, math, and instructions required to open apps, browse the web, and run the operating system.

🎮 GPU (Graphics Card)

The muscle! This special chip only does one thing: calculate insanely complex 3D math to draw video game graphics and render videos super fast.

🚀 RAM (Memory)

The computer's short-term workbench. It temporarily holds the apps and tabs you have open RIGHT NOW so the CPU can reach them instantly.

💾 NVMe SSD (Storage)

The digital vault. A super-fast flash drive where your operating system, downloaded games, and files are saved permanently, even when the power is off.

🔋 99Wh Li-Po Battery

A massive array of lithium-polymer cells. Laptops need HUGE batteries because big screens and powerful CPUs drink a lot of electricity.

❄️ Fans & Copper Heatpipes

CPUs and GPUs get burning hot! The orange copper pipes suck the heat away, and the spinning fans blast the hot air out the back vents.

🔊 Stereo Speakers

Large vibrating magnets located on the left and right sides to give you loud, immersive stereo sound for movies and games.

🔌 I/O Ports

The doors to the outside world! USB-C for charging and data, HDMI for plugging into TVs, and headphone jacks for private listening.
