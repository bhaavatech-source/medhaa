🕉️ Where this comes from
This session blends two traditional practices — a body-and-breath relaxation, and a gentle releasing of old mental patterns. The deepest form of this stillness is traditionally called chitta-vṛtti-nirodha, a mind free of fluctuation, described as a step toward moksha. You don't need to think about any of this for the practice to help — it's simply here for anyone curious where it comes from.
