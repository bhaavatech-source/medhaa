STEM Games for Kids

Explore free STEM games for kids that teach real engineering through hands-on building: design rockets, drones, cars, and devices using authentic components. These science and technology games build systems thinking, applied physics, and problem-solving skills used by real engineers, with no signup required.

Device Engineer

Assemble a smartphone or laptop from real, data-driven components.

Chip Detective

Explore the microscopic world of a computer chip and semiconductors.

Rocket Engineer

Design a sounding rocket or two-stage orbital rocket on a mission budget.

Bhava Space Academy

Explore a 3D ISS module by module and diagnose space emergencies.

Drone Engineer

Build an FPV racing drone or cinematic camera drone from real parts.

Car Designer

Pick real components with cost, weight, and reliability trade-offs.

Plane Builder

Assemble an aircraft piece by piece, learning what each part does.

Bike Builder

Explore full motorcycle builds and label 18 real parts each.

Build Cycles

Build a bicycle in progressive levels and diagnose real issues.
